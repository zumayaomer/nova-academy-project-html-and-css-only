FAQ

Common questions

Answers to the things people ask us most.

How fast do you reply?

Most messages get a reply within 24 hours on weekdays. On weekends it can take a bit longer.

Can I become a teacher on Nova Academy?

Yes! Choose "Become a teacher" in the subject dropdown, and tell us a little about your subject. We review every application.

Do you offer refunds?

Absolutely. If a course isn't right for you, we refund within 30 days — no questions asked.

Can my school use Nova Academy?

Yes. Our Classroom plan covers up to 40 students per teacher. Get in touch and we'll help you set it up.
